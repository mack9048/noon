Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Guests can open 2 free lessons in every subject, and paid courses have a 7-day free trial.

Which boards does Madrasa Support follow?

Sunni Vidyabhyasa Board (AP), Sunni Board (EK) and a General syllabus — from Class 1 to 10, +1 and +2.

Is it safe for young children?

Yes. Noon Buds is made only for children: no ads, no public chat and no strangers. Live classes are taken by verified usthads and parents can see every step.

Can our madrasa use Noon Buds for our own students?

Yes. Your madrasa gets its own space with admissions, batches, attendance, fees, exams and report cards, plus all our lesson content. Send us a message to book a demo.

How do I pay?

Pay inside the app by UPI, card or bank transfer. Madrasas can also collect fees from parents through the app.
