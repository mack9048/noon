Privacy Policy

Noon Learn is made for children and their families. We keep what we collect small, we never show ads, and we never sell anyone's information. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

{{wave:#F4F9FF}}

Who we are

Noon Learn (including the Noon Buds programme) runs the Noon Learn app and website at www.noonlearn.com. Questions about privacy: {{email}}.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, class, admission number, and a phone number or email used to log in. For children, a parent's name and phone number.
  • Learning records: lessons and videos watched, test marks, attendance, leave requests and homework a student sends in.
  • Fees: fee and payment records that an institution keeps for its own students (we do not store card details).
  • Messages: class chat between students, parents and teachers of the same institution.
  • Live classes: the camera and microphone are used only while a student or teacher is in a live class, and only if they allow it in their browser or phone. We do not record live classes unless the teacher uploads a recording as a lesson.
  • Nooni (our learning helper): the questions a person asks Nooni, their first name, role and institution name, so the answer fits. An AI service provider processes these to write the answer. Please do not type phone numbers or private details into Nooni.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to log people in, show a child's lessons and progress, let teachers and the institution office manage classes, attendance and fees, and keep the service safe. We do not show advertising, we do not track children across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

Who can see it

  • A student's own teachers and the office of the institution they belong to, and the child's parent or guardian.
  • Noon Learn staff who support the service.
  • Service providers that help us run the app under our instructions: web and server hosting, live video (LiveKit) and the AI service used by Nooni. They may not use the information for anything else.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Children

Children use Noon Learn through an institution or with a parent's permission. A parent or guardian can ask to see, correct or delete their child's information at any time (see below). We do not knowingly collect more than a child needs to learn.

How long we keep it

While the account is active and the institution needs the records (for example attendance and marks). When an account is deleted, we delete or anonymise its personal information, except records we must keep by law.

Delete your account or your child's account

Send an email to {{email}} from the email address or phone number on the account (or ask your institution's office). Write "Delete account" and the name of the student. We confirm who you are, then delete the account and its data within 30 days. Institution accounts can also be removed by the institution's own admin.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored in scrambled form. Each institution's data is kept separate from the others.

Changes and contact
